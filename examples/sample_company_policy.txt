Company Policy Handbook
Last updated: January 2026

1. VACATION POLICY
Employees are entitled to 15 paid vacation days per year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 5 days. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the direct manager. Employees with more than 3 years of tenure receive 20 vacation days per year.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required. Sick leave does not accumulate from year to year. Mental health days are included under sick leave and do not require a medical reason.

3. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote days must be coordinated with the team to ensure adequate office coverage on Mondays and Wednesdays. Fully remote positions are available only for roles listed as remote-first in the job description. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for equipment purchases.

4. HEALTH INSURANCE
The company offers two health insurance plans: Basic and Premium. The Basic plan covers 80% of medical expenses with a $500 deductible. The Premium plan covers 90% with a $200 deductible. Dental and vision coverage are included in both plans. Employees are eligible for health insurance after 30 days of employment.

5. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Primary caregivers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Employees must have been with the company for at least 6 months to qualify. Parental leave can be taken at any point within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption of a child.

6. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
The company provides an annual training budget of $2,000 per employee for conferences, courses, and certifications. Employees must obtain manager approval before registering for any program. Time off for training during work hours is allowed up to 5 days per year.

7. CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees must complete the annual Code of Conduct training. Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited. Confidential company information must not be shared with external parties. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
